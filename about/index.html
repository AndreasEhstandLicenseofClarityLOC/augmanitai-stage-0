About Andreas Ehstand

From court to code to compendium — the same analytical precision that decoded elite athletic performance now applied to decoding the unnamed phenomena of human-AI interaction.

Trajectory

25 years of professional sport coaching → multiple years as Bundesliga tennis coach → systematic performance factor analysis at elite level → doctoral training programme in quantitative methods at TU Dortmund's IFS → systematic observation methodology → transition into software development and AI research → AUGMANITAI Compendium with 6,000+ named phenomena for human-AI interaction.

Education

  • Research Associate (Wissenschaftlicher Mitarbeiter) at TU Dortmund — Lehrstuhl fuer Allgemeine Paedagogik, Prof. Dr. Sabine Hornberg. Trained in quantitative and qualitative empirical research methods.
  • Doctoral training programme at TU Dortmund's Institut fuer Schulentwicklungsforschung (IFS) — additional quantitative methods training.
  • Zertifikat Hochschullehre Bayern — one of the youngest holders at time of completion. Awarded by Fortbildungszentrum Hochschullehre Bayern (FBZHL).
  • Staatsexamen Bavaria (German, Sports, Informatics).
  • University-level teaching at TU Dortmund and University of Bayreuth across pedagogy, didactics, sport science, and performance factor analysis.
