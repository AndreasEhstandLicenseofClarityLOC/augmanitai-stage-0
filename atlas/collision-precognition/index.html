Collision Precognition

AUGMANITAI Generated
Advanced collision warning systems don't react to imminent impact; they predict impact trajectories and recognize when collision is inevitable, often microseconds before human perception catches up. Drivers report being jolted by warnings for dangers they hadn't yet registered, creating a sensation of the vehicle seeing dangers before they're consciously visible.
