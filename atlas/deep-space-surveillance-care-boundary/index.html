Deep Space Surveillance-Care Boundary

NEOMANITAI CORE
The ethical boundary between AI-driven health monitoring that supports astronaut wellbeing and continuous behavioral surveillance that undermines crew autonomy. Systems designed to detect anxiety, interpersonal conflict, or cognitive decline through voice analysis and behavioral patterns may be experienced as control rather than care — particularly on multi-year missions where crew cannot opt out. Space agencies acknowledge this tension but has no framework for resolving it.
