Educational AI Vendor Lock-In

Education AI
Schools adopting AI platforms that create switching costs — learner data, curriculum integration, teacher training, parent accounts all locked in. The vendor optimizes for retention. The school optimizes for learning. The objectives diverge. The contract lasts 5 years.
