Morphological Hallucination

MEGA DOMAINS
A phenomenon where neural models generate or recognize non-existent morphological structures, inflections, or derivations. Occurs when the model's morphological knowledge derived from statistical patterns conflicts with actual language rules, particularly for rare or novel word forms.
