§29 Re-Contextualization, Not Original-Priority Claim. Within the AUGMANITAI Framework, terms may share lexical roots or domain-naming conventions with established public-domain terminology (e.g. mathematical algorithms, anatomical references, standard engineering practices, generic scientific terms). Such surface-level lexical overlap does NOT constitute a claim of original-priority origination over those public-domain concepts. The framework re-contextualizes observable phenomena within its phenomenological lens; the underlying public-domain concepts remain attributable to their original communities of practice. Where a definition discusses observable patterns at the intersection of human cognition and AI behaviour, the descriptive contribution is the AUGMANITAI framing — not the underlying field-standard concepts being framed. No term in this corpus constitutes architectural specification, system-design requirement, or implementation guidance for any technical system; phenomenological descriptions are observations, not blueprints.
