AUGMANITAI Compendium - Research Domains

Within the AUGMANITAI Framework, terminology is organized across 169 research domain clusters, totalling 35852 defined terms. The framework is created and maintained by Andreas Ehstand under Independent Research - Applied Terminology Science.

Featured Domains

Subjective Phenomenology of Human-AI Interaction

8063 terms - cluster RPH

Phenomenological terms describing subjective experience in human-AI interaction.

AUGMANITAI Compendium Core

1816 terms - cluster AUG

Core terms of the AUGMANITAI Compendium - the descriptive terminology of human-AI interaction.

NEOMANITAI Knowledge Graph

2439 terms - cluster NEO

Knowledge-graph layer cross-referencing terms across the AUGMANITAI ecosystem.

Cross-Domain Terminology Pool

2553 terms - cluster WK

Cross-disciplinary terms spanning multiple research domains.

ROBMANITAI

297 related terms - Robotics Branch

Robotics Human-AI Interaction Phenomenology, extending to multi-robot coordination, human-robot team dynamics, and multi-agent / agent-team orchestration.

PERMANITAI

887 related terms - Performance Branch

Substrate-Independent Performance Factor framework. Cross-substrate performance analysis grounded in 25-year sport-science methodology.

EDUMANITAI

1,419 related terms - Education Branch

Phenomenology of learning, pedagogy, and educational human-AI interaction. Grounded in TU Dortmund pedagogical research lineage.

JOBMANITAI

1,463 related terms - Work Branch

Phenomenology of work patterns shaped by AI integration: professional identity shifts, workflow re-mediation, role evolution.
