⚡ Context · Why terminology matters
  • Unnamed = hard to share. Millions interact with AI daily and experience phenomena that lack clear names. Shared terminology enables precise comparison, discussion, and systematic research.
  • Language creates clarity. When something can be named precisely, it can be understood precisely. Terms transform vague experiences into recognizable, describable patterns — across disciplines, across borders.
  • No vocabulary, no science. Scientific fields develop terminology to organize knowledge. Human-AI interaction currently lacks widely adopted vocabulary. This compendium provides systematic terminology for the field.
  • Recognition enables discussion. Reading a term and thinking 'this happens too' — that moment of recognition is where shared understanding begins.
